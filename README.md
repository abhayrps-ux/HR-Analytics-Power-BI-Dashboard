# HR-Analytics-Power-BI-Dashboard
Interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset. Includes DAX measures, Power Query transformations, KPI analysis, and interactive visualizations for workforce insights.
📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

📌 **Project Overview**

This project presents an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI and the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset.

The dashboard helps HR teams analyze employee attrition, workforce distribution, salary trends, job satisfaction, and department performance through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered

- How many employees are currently in the company?
- What is the employee attrition rate?
- Which department has the highest attrition?
- Which job role has the highest employee turnover?
- What is the average monthly income?
- Which education field has the most employees?
- How satisfied are employees across departments?

## 💡 Key Insights

- Overall employee attrition is **16.12%**.
- Research & Development has the highest employee count.
- Sales department experiences higher attrition than Human Resources.
- Laboratory Technicians have the highest employee exits.
- Life Sciences is the most common education field.
